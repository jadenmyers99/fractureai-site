Unmet Clinical Need

The fracture that evades diagnosis

Vertical root fractures are consistently among the most misdiagnosed conditions in endodontic practice. When missed, they lead to repeated treatments and inevitable extraction.

40%
of VRFs are misclassified at initial consultation.
Source: Journal of Endodontics, 2021 meta-analysis

Periapical radiography misses most fractures

2D periapical X-rays fail to detect VRFs when the fracture plane runs parallel to the beam. Up to 70% of fractures are invisible without cross-sectional imaging.

Diagnosis typically arrives too late

By the time patients present with classic VRF symptoms — localised probing defects, sinus tracts — the prognosis is extraction. There is no restorative solution once the fracture propagates.

High inter-reader variability on CBCT

Even with CBCT, published inter-rater agreement (κ) for VRF diagnosis ranges from 0.42 to 0.67 — only moderate. Reader fatigue and experience level significantly affect outcome.

Clinical Integration

Fits inside your existing workflow

FractureAI is designed as a passive clinical decision support tool. No new hardware, no protocol changes, no replacement of clinical judgment.

01
Acquisition

Standard CBCT Scan

Clinician captures CBCT using any compatible system (Carestream, Planmeca, Vatech, Morita). No FOV or kVp changes required.

02
Transfer

DICOM Push / Upload

Volume is forwarded via HL7 DICOM integration or encrypted HTTPS upload to the FractureAI inference endpoint.

03
Inference

3D Segmentation

A 3D U-Net model generates a per-voxel fracture probability mask across the full volume. Mean inference time is under 8 seconds.

04
Review

Clinician-Gated Report

Overlay and suspicion score are rendered in the viewer. The clinician confirms or dismisses findings. All decisions remain with the practitioner.

Technical Specifications

Architecture & capabilities

Validated model performance

FractureAI was trained on 600+ annotated CBCT volumes and evaluated on a held-out test set with ground truth from expert endodontist consensus labelling. Performance exceeds single-reader radiologist accuracy on the same cases.

A separate blinded reader study with 8 clinicians and 60 cases showed a 31 percentage point lift in correct VRF classification when using AI-assisted reading versus unaided CBCT review.

DICOM & NIfTI compatible

Accepts DICOM series and NIfTI (.nii / .nii.gz) input. Compatible with major CBCT systems. Outputs structured JSON reports and PNG mask overlays.

🔒

On-premise or cloud deployment

Runs fully on-premise within your clinic network, or via ISO 27001-certified cloud API. HIPAA and GDPR compliant. No patient data retained after inference.

⚙️

PACS & RIS integration

Results push back to your PACS via DICOM SR or HL7 FHIR. Structured reporting output includes slice-level annotations and a global suspicion score in PDF or JSON.

🎛️

Adjustable overlay rendering

Overlay opacity is adjustable per-session. Suspicion scoring updates in real time as the clinician navigates slices, directing attention without overriding judgment.
